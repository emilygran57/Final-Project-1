Threats to Ecosystems

Ecosystems face pressure from many directions at once. These are the five biggest drivers of nature loss identified by scientists.

🚜

Habitat Loss

Clearing land for farms, roads and cities is the leading cause of species decline worldwide.

🎣

Overexploitation

Overfishing, overhunting and unsustainable logging remove species faster than they can recover.

🌡️

Climate Change

Rising temperatures bleach coral reefs, shift species ranges and intensify droughts and wildfires.

🧴

Pollution

Plastics, pesticides and fertilizer runoff poison wildlife and create "dead zones" in lakes and seas.

🐸

Invasive Species

Non-native plants and animals can outcompete local species and spread disease.

There is good news

Ecosystems can recover. Restored wetlands, replanted forests and protected marine areas have brought species back from the brink.
