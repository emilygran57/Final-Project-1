Ecosystems of the World

Every ecosystem is a community of living things interacting with their physical environment. Here are some of the most important ones on Earth.

🌳

Forests

Forests cover about 31% of land and are home to most land-based species. They absorb carbon dioxide, prevent erosion and regulate rainfall.

🪸

Coral Reefs

Reefs cover less than 1% of the ocean floor but support about a quarter of all marine species, and they protect coastlines from storms.

🦆

Wetlands

Marshes, swamps and bogs filter pollutants, soak up floodwater and store huge amounts of carbon in their soils.

🌾

Grasslands

Prairies and savannas support grazing animals and pollinators, and their deep roots build some of the most fertile soils on Earth.

🌊

Oceans

The ocean produces much of our oxygen, absorbs heat and carbon, and feeds billions of people.

🏜️

Deserts

Deserts host specially adapted plants and animals and contain fragile soil crusts that keep dust and erosion in check.

Keystone species

Some species have an outsized effect on their ecosystem. Remove them and the whole system can change.

🐺 Gray Wolves

After wolves returned to Yellowstone, elk grazing patterns shifted and streamside willows and aspens began to recover.

🦦 Sea Otters

Otters eat sea urchins, which keeps urchins from wiping out kelp forests that shelter fish.

🐝 Bees

Bees and other pollinators let flowering plants reproduce, supporting both wild habitats and farms.
