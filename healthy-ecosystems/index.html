What makes an ecosystem healthy?

A healthy ecosystem is one that can sustain itself over time and recover from disturbances like storms, droughts or fires. Scientists look for a few key signs:

🦋

Biodiversity

A wide variety of plants, animals, fungi and microbes, each filling a role in the food web.

💧

Clean Water

Rivers, lakes and groundwater free from excess nutrients, sediment and pollution.

🌱

Rich Soil

Living soil that stores carbon, holds water and recycles nutrients for new growth.

🔄

Resilience

The ability to bounce back after a disturbance instead of collapsing into a degraded state.

Why it matters

Nature provides "ecosystem services" that people depend on every day.

~50%of the oxygen we breathe comes from the ocean
75%of food crops rely at least partly on pollinators
1/3of the world's largest cities get drinking water from protected forests
